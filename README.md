### 🧩 Featured Projects

#### 🤖 AI agents

**🎬 [Reelsmith](https://github.com/harshal0001/reelsmith)**: `Python · FastAPI · NVIDIA Nemotron · LangChain · Remotion`<br/>
Turns a question into a researched, narrated, animated one-minute vertical reel. Tavily Deep Research gathers sources, Nemotron writes a two-character dialogue, Chatterbox Turbo voices it, WhisperX times every word, and independent scene agents animate six scenes that can each be revised on their own.

**🏭 [Procurement Supplier Lens](https://github.com/harshal0001/procurement-supplier-lens)**: `Next.js · FastAPI · Claude · Exa · ElevenLabs`<br/>
Explainable supplier risk scoring for small and mid-sized manufacturers. It extracts facts from supplier PDFs (each with a verbatim quote and page number), checks public news, scores risk with deterministic rules, and answers spoken questions in English or German with cited sources. A human approves every recommended action.

**🔬 [PaperScout](https://github.com/harshal0001/paperscout)**: `Python · NVIDIA Nemotron · Nebius · Europe PMC`<br/>
A research-paper discovery agent for healthcare and life sciences. It plans PICO-based Boolean searches, retrieves papers from Europe PMC, ranks every abstract, and writes an evidence summary where each claim is cited, with forest plots and evidence gap maps. Built for the Nebius Build Week hackathon.

**🛍️ [AI Storefront Design Studio](https://github.com/harshal0001/ai-storefront-design-studio)**: `Python · React · AWS Bedrock · Strands Agents · DynamoDB` · [Live demo](https://samplefrontendfromdemo.vercel.app)<br/>
A conversational AI designer that turns a merchant's plain-language description into complete storefront themes with a live preview, version history and undo/redo. Colour palettes are validated for WCAG contrast, and it deploys serverlessly on Bedrock AgentCore with AWS CDK.

#### 🏛️ European standards &amp; compliance

**🧾 [XRechnung Validator](https://github.com/harshal0001/xrechnung-validator)**: `Python · lxml · Saxon (Schematron) · React` · [Live](https://xrechnung.harshalkothari.tech)<br/>
Validates German e-invoices (XRechnung, ZUGFeRD, UBL, CII) against EN 16931 and the KoSIT rule set, then explains each failure in plain German or English: what is wrong, why it matters, and how to fix it.

**🔋 [Batteriepass](https://github.com/harshal0001/batteriepass)**: `Python · PyTorch · ONNX · SAMM · Pydantic`<br/>
A digital asset health passport. It builds Catena-X battery passports, checks them against Annex XIII of EU Battery Regulation 2023/1542, and estimates state of health and remaining useful life with calibrated uncertainty and drift warnings. A second profile applies the same method to turbofan engines.

**🛣️ [DATEX II Road-Weather Adapter](https://github.com/harshal0001/datex2-road-weather-adapter)**: `Python · FastAPI · xsdata · Leaflet`<br/>
Fuses Bavaria's road-weather sources (SWS road stations, DWD, LoRaWAN sensors, OpenWeatherMap) field by field with full provenance and emits XSD-validated **DATEX II v3** that any European traffic centre can consume. New sources plug in as one class, and new regions as one YAML profile.
